CSP-GAMES

Your phone is the controller.

Pick a game on this screen, scan the QR code with your phone, and play.

Tennis

Swing your phone like a racket on a 3D court. Play the computer or a friend.

Ping Pong

Same swing, table-tennis rules. First to 11 points wins.

How to play

  1. Choose a game above and keep this screen open.
  2. Scan the QR code with your phone camera.
  3. On the phone: tap Enable motion, calibrate, then Ready.
  4. Start the match from this screen.
